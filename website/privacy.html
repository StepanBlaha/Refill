Privacy, in plain words

Last updated 2026-09-30

Short version: Refill runs on your Mac and stays there. The developer collects nothing. There is no Refill server, no account, no analytics and no telemetry.

What we collect

Nothing. The developer of Refill does not receive, store or process any of your data. There is no backend for it to reach.

What the app reads on your Mac

To show your usage, Refill reads sign-in information that your other tools already keep locally:

  • Claude Code: the login stored in your macOS Keychain, and the Claude config folders (~/.claude, any ~/.claude-*, and folders you add in Settings). Refreshed tokens are written back to the same Keychain item.
  • Codex CLI: rate-limit data from your local session logs in ~/.codex/sessions.
  • Gemini, Cursor and GitHub Copilot: the local sign-in tokens those tools store on your Mac.

These credentials are used only to ask the provider for your own usage numbers. They are never sent anywhere else.

Who the app talks to

Only the AI providers' own services, using your own login, and only what is needed to read usage:

  • api.anthropic.com for Claude usage
  • console.anthropic.com to refresh an expired Claude token
  • api.github.com for Copilot usage
  • cursor.com for Cursor usage
  • Google's cloudcode-pa endpoint for Gemini quota

Plus any integration you set up yourself (ntfy, Pushover, Telegram, Discord, Slack, Home Assistant, Hue, WLED, or a custom webhook). Those receive the event text and color you configured, at the address you chose, and nothing more. Their handling of that data is up to them.

Files Refill creates

Everything lives in ~/.config/refill: settings, integrations.json (permissions 0600, since it may hold your webhook URLs or bot tokens), status.json and events.jsonl. Delete the folder and it is all gone.

Local dashboard

Refill serves a small dashboard at 127.0.0.1:7788, reachable only from your Mac. If you switch on "Visible on Wi-Fi", other devices on your local network can open it. That is off by default, and it never goes to the internet.

Analytics and tracking

None. No analytics, no crash reporting, no advertising IDs, no cookies. This website sets no cookies either. Fonts are your system fonts, so nothing is loaded from third parties.

Your control

Remove any integration, turn off the dashboard, or delete ~/.config/refill at any time. Quitting and deleting the app ends everything it does.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it.
